# Q6: disclosed multi-operation replay contexts

The historical word-ranking test partition was already covered by lexical article and target-context entries, but its newly replayed typed forms and output surfaces were not fully represented in the control registry. The two-run current-product screen exposed those forms; they cannot be treated as new independent controls.

The public source archive and the recorded replay hashes were checked before deriving a deterministic, versioned [disclosure fixture](../../tools/typing_experiments/wordrank-replay-disclosure-v1/NOTICE.md). It contains 3,419 distinct article/prefix/token combinations from the 1,800 archived rows: source inputs, synthetic targets and actual boundary outputs, including the actual prefix after mechanical input handling. It rejects input identity mismatch, duplicate rows, a changed preceding prefix and malformed boundary output. The source and observations were rechecked after reading. Two independent captures matched byte for byte. The fixture is derived from public Wikipedia sources; it does not include owner messages or assert the correctness of any label.

A control-registry entry records 3,419 regression keys, 2,535 surface keys and 402 source-article keys. A separate lexical entry records 3,394 normalized context keys. Compared with the prior registries, 2,480 surface and 1,593 normalized context keys are new. The other entries and incomplete coverage declarations remain unchanged. Both proposed registries were generated twice with identical bytes; actual old/new probes confirmed that a new context and a new surface, including the surface with a different prefix, are refused by the updated real gates. Two focused tests cover source/observed forms and malformed, duplicated or altered inputs. A CI check rebuilds both entries from the tracked disclosure fixture and verifies source and receipt bindings without `build/`; it does not re-execute the 1,800-row product replay. Full historical provenance, global coverage and fresh independent qualification remain open.

The broader Python profiles after this disclosure passed 790 standard-library tests with no skips and 79 scientific tests with one explicit MLX-environment skip; these runs preceded the subsequent three-context packed-candidate addendum.
