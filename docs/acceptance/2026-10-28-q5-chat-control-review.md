# Q5: post-hoc source-control review

The first Russian chat-shaped baseline changed eleven source controls beyond
case alone. Manual review marks eight occurrences of recognizable colloquial
forms for preservation in this regression set: `пейринг`, `токсиков`, `админку`,
`забанили`, `бадлон` (two contexts), `могёт` and `ебобо`. Their observed
outputs and exact public source IDs are in the versioned review file. Three
other source forms are marked as possible original typos and are not counted as
false corrections. These are editorial judgments made after seeing the output;
they do not establish the authors' actual intent or estimate population error.

The audit binds each case to the disclosed source row and validates the original
observer result, editor flags and Undo through the shared observation checker.
It requires that all eleven non-case-only source-control changes are accounted
for. All eleven source article identities are already in the permanent lexical
exclusions. The remaining 9,532 source-control occurrences were not individually
reviewed; no whole-corpus false-change rate is claimed. No runtime policy,
model, APK or phone was changed.

The [numeric receipt](2026-10-28-q5-chat-control-review-evidence.json) binds
rows, observer output, review labels and audit code. Missing cases, changed
baseline outputs and mismatched source words fail the audit. The cases are
regression-only and cannot qualify a new policy or serve as a fresh holdout.
The separate [comparison](2026-10-28-q5-chat-control-comparison.json) reports
that the observed baseline preserved zero of eight reviewed forms; it can be
rerun against later observer results without requiring the old output. A case
change alone is not counted as a spelling failure in this comparison.

A [local rare-word overlay experiment](2026-10-28-q5-chat-guard-experiment.md)
preserved all eight occurrences in the disclosed replay, with seven other
synthetic typo outputs changing to the original input. It was not packaged or
qualified for use in Rune.
