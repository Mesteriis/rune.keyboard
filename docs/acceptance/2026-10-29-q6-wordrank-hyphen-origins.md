# Q6: source origins of the three unresolved word-ranking surfaces

All five rows with the three dictionary-unknown targets in the retained 9,000-row word-ranking corpus were matched to their original articles in the exact pinned Wikipedia shard. Each archived prefix plus target occurred once in its article. The target was the first component of a hyphenated source form: three occurrences of `прайм-тайм`, one `кибер-девушки` and one `галло-романской`. The historical selector's word regex extracted the left fragment; it did not preserve the full source token in these target fields.

The pinned dictionary has no dictionary-only analysis for the three standalone heads or for these full compound surfaces. The known analyses of the second components are already blocked in the lexical registry, and the exact unknown heads remain quarantined. This source evidence explains the historical fragments; it does not invent canonical lemmas or establish that a partial token is a valid independent target in ordinary typing.

The five source-attested [contexts](../../tools/typing_experiments/wordrank-hyphen-origin-v1/NOTICE.md) were added to both registries. The control entry protects three complete compound surfaces and their five article/context combinations; the lexical entry protects the five normalized complete-compound contexts. The real gates reject all five source-derived cases, including a changed-prefix surface probe. Other entries and incomplete coverage declarations are unchanged. Two source-audit runs and two registry builds were byte-identical; source bytes, archive rows, dictionary receipt and audit identity are bound in the [numeric receipt](2026-10-29-q6-wordrank-hyphen-source.json). The tracked CI check verifies the retained source/receipt bindings and pre-existing component exclusions without needing the local Wikipedia shard. Re-running the source audit itself requires that exact pinned shard.

This closes the source-token explanation for these five archived rows, not the overall family/provenance inventory or independent Q6 qualification. No model, packaged asset or product policy was changed.
