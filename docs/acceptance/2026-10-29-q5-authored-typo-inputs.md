# Q5 authored dialogue deletion inputs

From the 20 previously disclosed punctuation-stripped author-written phrases,
19 have a final word of at least five letters. Each receives one deterministic
interior deletion in that final word. Both typed and intended target contexts,
and all earlier phrase-stage inputs, are registered in the two public ledgers
before product replay. The 94 registered row occurrences include both variants
at the last boundary; the 19 intended labels are the original author words.

Some mutated forms may themselves be valid words. These are disclosed synthetic
regression inputs, not observations of real typing errors or an independent
qualification. Registry mutation, overlap and no-`build/` tests pass. Any newly
observed output contexts must be disclosed separately before treating the replay
as a reusable regression result.
