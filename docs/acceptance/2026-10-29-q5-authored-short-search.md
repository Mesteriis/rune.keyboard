# Q5 disclosed short-word suggestion search

A label-free, model-free current-product token observer replayed three previously
registered synthetic deletion inputs with their disclosed phrase prefixes. It
starts from a prefilled prefix, not a complete multiword typing session. The
source fixture contains the three timing-free observations, the capture receipt
hash and repository source/asset bindings. Two runs produced identical
projections; no external model was called.

The ordinary three-candidate lists were `метр, меть, место` for `мето`,
`двух, двое, двоих` for `двох`, and `собой, собр, соей` for `собй`. The
seven-item expanded manual list contained intended `метро` at position five,
`двоих` at position three and `собой` at position one. The general-local
search was `UNAVAILABLE` with zero inspected states in all three. The ordinary
search completed; this is not evidence that an incomplete search hit a budget.

Before reuse as regression material, 24 distinct input, intended-target and
observed candidate contexts are registered in both ledgers. The checker binds
the frozen projection, source fixture and deterministic derivation without
`build/`. These previously disclosed, synthetic observations are not an
independent accuracy test, do not establish full-session or physical-device
behavior and do not qualify an automatic admission or ranking change.
