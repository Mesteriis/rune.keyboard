unfinished/unresolved retention labels and all 7,484 correct-original controls.

## Next evidence-driven work

Eighteen lost old correct proposals have a possible genitive reading on the
immediate right noun; many also have dative or nominative alternatives. This is
an association in the error audit, not proof of a particular causal split. The
new noun-complement training has introduced useful retention evidence without
enough matched positive predicate/complement contrasts. Add authored valid
predicate repairs with oblique/homographic complements and a supporting left
copula, while preserving genuine instrumental retention. Ordinary-finite/full
adjective coverage and genuine repairs must remain in the training objective.
Do not respond by removing hard categories, hard-locking nouns, or merely
repeating this fit unchanged.
