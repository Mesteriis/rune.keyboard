# Q5: Common Voice spontaneous Russian source screen

The [Mozilla Common Voice Spontaneous Speech 5.0 Russian datasheet](https://mozilladatacollective.com/datasets/cmu5mg3pr00simh07epeylc55)
identifies release `sps-corpus-5.0-2026-09-11` as 783 recorded answers from
21 speakers, with 456 transcribed and validated clips and 85 pending. It reports
CC0-1.0, while its stated forbidden usage also prohibits re-hosting or
re-sharing the dataset and attempting to identify speakers. Its intended uses
are speech recognition and certain language-learning or revitalisation uses;
this screen does not resolve whether the proposed Rune evaluation is permitted
under all applicable terms.

The TSV description includes a hashed contributor ID and transcription, so
speaker-level clustering appears technically possible if those fields are
available as described. This is an inference from the schema, not a validation
of participant identity, transcription accuracy, or independence. Spoken
answers to prompts are also not typed everyday chat, and the reported 21-speaker
sample cannot establish a broadly representative author-clustered baseline.
The clip count is not the number of suitable short controls.

No dataset was downloaded, inspected, redistributed or used for evaluation.
Neither the license label alone nor the datasheet clears the explicit
re-sharing restriction. The source therefore does not currently satisfy the
review's reproducible distributable Q5 corpus, and this metadata screen does
not close Q5, establish a fresh holdout or report a false-change rate.
