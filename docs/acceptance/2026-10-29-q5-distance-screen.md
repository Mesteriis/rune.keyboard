# Q5: disclosed screen of higher-distance automatic choices

A source-bound offline analyzer screened the previously recorded 34,330-row Tatoeba token replay. For each automatic edit it tries to match the controller's recorded admission candidate to the candidate generation retained with the edit, then groups matched candidates by the recorded `unitDistance`. This is a post-hoc screen of recorded outcomes, not a replay of a changed policy or independent qualification.

The 9,543 unproofread source controls had 41 automatic edits; 11 had a matching recorded candidate, of which three were more than one edit away. Those three are the already reviewed `пейринг`, `токсиков` and `админку` changes. Thirty other control edits were not matched to this candidate field and are excluded from the distance breakdown. Source spelling is not by itself proof of intent.

Of 24,787 synthetically edited rows, 16,778 had an automatic edit. The analyzer matched 16,767 of those to a recorded candidate. Among the matched candidates, 91 were more than one edit away and none matched the synthetic target: 19 doubled-consonant, six removed-sign, 27 е/и and 39 о/а cases. Eleven edits could not be matched and are not classified. The generator applies one simple orthographic operation per row, so the absence of correct higher-distance choices is expected for this corpus and says nothing about real multi-error typing.

A distance veto is therefore only a hypothesis: changing the ranking/admission cascade could select a different candidate, and the sample provides no evidence about legitimate corrections requiring multiple edits or other sources. No threshold, model, packaged asset or product behavior was changed. Two runs of the analyzer produced byte-identical results and matched the archived replay's source and observation hashes. Two focused tests check attribution, unclassified edits, identity and corrupt input. The [numeric receipt](2026-10-29-q5-distance-screen.json) binds the archived rows, replay observations, replay receipt and analyzer; original data remain under `build/q5-chat-v1-*`. Independent family/article-separated controls, multi-error examples and actual candidate replay are still required before a policy decision.
