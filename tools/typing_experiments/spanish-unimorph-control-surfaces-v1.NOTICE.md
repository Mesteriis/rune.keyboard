# Spanish UniMorph exclusion fixture

The source is [UniMorph Spanish](https://github.com/unimorph/spa/tree/b9655efb0e5ce0f99809badaa7f4e93017ab8430),
which identifies English Wiktionary as its source and declares CC BY-SA 3.0.
The fixture retains headword titles and forms for attribution to Wiktionary
contributors through page histories. For each `sourceTitles` title, its history
is available at `https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/<URL-encoded-title>?action=history`.
The projected fixture is distributed under CC BY-SA 3.0. It is a conservative exact-form exclusion,
not an assertion of intended sense, complete family coverage or qualification.
