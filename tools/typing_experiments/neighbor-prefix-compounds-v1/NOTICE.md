# Historical neighbor-prefix compounds

These contexts reproduce complete, interior compound or mixed-script tokens
already disclosed in the historical public neighbor corpus. The prefix and
article identities come from `neighbor-history-v1`; the source audit checks
all captured prefixes against its exact pinned 2023-11-01 Russian Wikipedia
shards. Articles and attribution links are in that corpus's `articles.jsonl`.
Wikipedia source data is distributed under CC BY-SA 3.0 and GFDL; see the
[Wikimedia notice](../../model/rune-text-0.2/WIKIMEDIA_NOTICE.md).

Tokens at uncertain prefix edges are excluded. These disclosures are not an
independent evaluation set, and no morphological family is inferred from them.
